# Debrief: Mike call, July 21 2026 (role realignment + acquisition review)

The scheduled Tuesday call with Mike. Two halves: (1) Matt's role at Cool Hollow Solutions, and (2) a walk-through of the client acquisition worksheet and the ACE model draft.

## The big shift: Matt's role is now undefined

- The CHS restructure has grown from "film a few community lessons" into a full strategic vision exercise. Mark is now thinking three-year big picture, which is good for the firm but left Matt's role floating.
- Mike's blunt diagnosis: the team is overworked and runs ad hoc with no structure/systems, so they want someone to take **workload off them**, not someone bringing a big strategic idea. A young (25) new guy arriving with a big vision reads as an **irritation**, not a solution. Not personal, structural. Matt lacks the tenure/credibility to push strategy through a busy team, no matter how good the idea.
- The community "product owner" box only becomes logically Matt's **after** the bigger vision is agreed (months out, likely past his visa date). Until then there is no clean box that's his.
- Mike's prescribed play: for the next couple of months, make yourself useful tactically, take tasks off Kathy, Hannah, and Cam, join the team Zooms, be the guy who makes their week easier. That's job security while the vision forms, and it closes the value-perception gap. Reiterate you want to own the community piece once the vision lands.
- Visa reality: Matt's 10-year US visa caps him at 6 months per stay; current stay ends ~end of October, so early November is the exit. Option floated: leave, keep working with Mike remotely (from London, where his mum is), come back to relaunch the community once the vision is solved. Mike leaned toward "stay and add value now" as plan A, "leave and come back" as the dignified fallback.
- Mike explicitly warned about the cost/value gap: if Matt isn't visibly adding value, the risk is Mark thinking "are we paying this guy, and what is he doing?"
