This notice explains what happens to the information you provide when
using Cool Hollow Coaching's Tools (the Reclaim Protocol, Impact Map,
Monday Morning Dashboard, Profit Discovery Audit, Cash Confidence,
Bottleneck Breakthrough Plan, Team Builder, and the Hidden-Profit
Analyzer).

## Our policy: nothing is stored

**We do not retain the files you upload or the reports these Tools
generate.** Each Tool reads your uploaded file into memory only long
enough to build your results, displays them to you on screen, and lets
you download a PDF copy for your own records. Nothing is written to a
database, saved to a server, or kept once your session ends. Closing
the browser tab without downloading means the results are gone. Your
downloaded PDF is the only copy that exists, and it exists only on your
own device.

This was confirmed technically on 2026-07-09: none of the seven
milestone tools write uploaded data or generated results to disk or to
a database. Verified directly against the code.

## What this means in practice

- **Upload a file, get your results, download the PDF.** That download
  is the only record.
- **If you lose the PDF, the results cannot be recovered** by Cool
  Hollow Coaching, they were never stored. You would need to re-upload
  your original file and run the Tool again.
- **We recommend keeping your own copies** of both the file you upload
  and the PDF you download, the same way you'd keep any financial
  document.
