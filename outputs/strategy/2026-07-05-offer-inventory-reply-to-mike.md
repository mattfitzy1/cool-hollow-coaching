# Reply to Mike: Offer Inventory (In-House / Partner x Event-Based / Cyclical)

Drafted July 5, 2026, for Matt to review and send himself ahead of the Monday discussion.

---

Mike,

Love this framing. Monthly pricing with levels makes sense to me, especially the transition point you called out. Someone finishes the 12 weeks, and instead of a goodbye, there's a natural next step into the community at a monthly rate. That's where the real lifetime value is anyway.

I sat down and made the list. I tried to be exhaustive like you asked, so some of these are built and ready, some are things we could stand up fast with the team we have, and a few are further out. I marked the ones that already exist. Here's everything:

**IN-HOUSE, EVENT-BASED**

1. Business Without You, the 12-week flagship program (built, all 7 milestone tools live and tested)
2. Beta cohort runs of Milestones 1-3 live on Zoom (the pre-filming validation we talked about)
3. A standalone Profit Discovery session with Cam. P&L teardown, one sitting, walk out with a dollar figure. Could work as a paid diagnostic or a low-ticket front-end offer
4. A Dashboard Install sprint. We build the owner's five-metric Monday dashboard with them in a week
5. A 13-Week Cash Forecast build session with Cam, one intensive working session
6. A Hiring Sprint with Hannah. Scorecard, job description, and interview plan for one key role
7. A 1:1 strategy day with Mark. Premium ticket, one owner, full day
8. Quarterly planning workshop. Owners come in, leave with their next 90 days mapped
9. An annual live event or in-person mastermind day once we have enough alumni to fill a room
10. The Liberation Finale as its own capstone event, could eventually be something alumni come back for

**IN-HOUSE, CYCLICAL**

1. The alumni community. Monthly membership after the 12 weeks, this is the transition offer you described
2. Weekly or biweekly live group Q&A calls with Mark (his domain per our curriculum rules)
3. Monthly CFO office hours with Cam
4. Monthly hot-seat calls, one owner in the chair, the group works their problem
5. Ongoing access to the seven milestone tools as a monthly software layer. They keep the dashboard, the cash forecast, the audit, all of it, as long as they're a member
6. Monthly accountability check-ins against their dashboard numbers
7. Quarterly business reviews with the team, a deeper version of the monthly rhythm
8. New content drops. A monthly case study or lesson, keeps the community fed without Mark filming constantly
9. The Tier 2 community for $10M+ owners, 1-2 live sessions a week with the full team, this is the big cyclical offer long term
10. An annual refresher pass through the milestones for alumni whose businesses have changed

**PARTNER-DELIVERED, EVENT-BASED**

1. Guest speaker masterclasses, one-off sessions from operators in our network
2. Tax strategy sessions with an outside tax specialist (the Tier 2 promise)
3. A business valuation assessment. What is this business actually worth without you in it. Very on-message for us
4. Exit and succession planning workshop through a legal or M&A partner
5. A one-time books cleanup or financial audit through a partner accounting firm, for owners whose numbers are too messy to run our tools on
6. Insurance and benefits review as a one-time audit event

**PARTNER-DELIVERED, CYCLICAL**

1. Fractional CFO or ongoing bookkeeping through a partner firm, for owners who want the Milestone 4 and 5 work done for them every month
2. Ongoing tax planning on retainer (Tier 2 core)
3. Investment and wealth management for the profit we help them find, through a licensed partner
4. Recruiting on retainer through a staffing partner, the ongoing version of the Hiring Sprint
5. Peer advisory boards run by trained outside facilitators once we're past what Mark can personally chair
6. Ongoing systems support (CRM, automation) through an implementation partner for owners coming out of the Break the Constraint milestone

A pattern I noticed writing this out: almost every event-based item has a natural cyclical twin. Profit Discovery becomes monthly CFO office hours. The Hiring Sprint becomes recruiting on retainer. The dashboard build becomes the monthly accountability rhythm. That maps cleanly onto your point about modular pricing levels. The events are the entry doors and the cyclical stuff is where people land and stay.

The partner categories are the thinnest right now because we haven't built that network yet. That might be worth a few minutes Monday too: which partner relationships to line up first.
