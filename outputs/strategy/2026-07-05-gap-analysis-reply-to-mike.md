**2. Pricing and monetization.** Not locked yet, still deciding lump sum versus monthly. We need tiered monthly pricing where each tier has a real reason to exist and an obvious next step up. This is the main thing I want to work through with you Monday.

**3. Partner network and loyalty.** Right now it's just Mark, Cam our CFO, and Hannah on hiring. That's the team, not partners. Where we need to get: you're a potential partner for content and delivery. Further out, certified coaches trained in Mark's methodology, something like the Simon Sinek model. Genuine question for Monday: what role do you want to play here, and should we put a formal arrangement around it?

**4. Customization.** One program, same for every owner right now. We need some flex by industry, an HVAC room, a glazing room, a general blue-collar room inside the community, and by tier, where the higher tiers get more direct access and more tailored coaching. Core content stays standard either way. Customization lives in the coaching layer, not the curriculum.

**5. Use of data and behavioral influence.** We have none right now. No visibility into who's engaging and who isn't. We need to track logins, how far people get through each milestone, who actually completes the Do worksheets, who's using the tools. That's what tells us who's about to drop off and who's ready to move up to Tier 2. You mentioned community profile completion and number of connections as early signals, I want to build around those.

**6. Management approach.** Not defined yet. We need someone, me at first, reviewing engagement data weekly and stepping in when someone goes quiet. This is relationship management, not customer service.

**7. Journey management.** No mapped journey exists today. We need a clear path: stranger to community member to program buyer to graduate to mentor, every drop-off point named and a plan to plug it. I know this is the specific workshop you want to run with me, I'm ready for it.

**8. Offering analytics.** Nothing built yet. We need a simple dashboard: completion rate per milestone, community engagement, free-to-paid conversion, and Tier 1 to Tier 2 upsell rate. Doesn't need to be complicated, five numbers, checked weekly. Same principle as the Monday dashboard we already built into the program itself.

Let's use Monday to get real traction on pricing and the partner question, those feel like the two that are actually blocking progress.
