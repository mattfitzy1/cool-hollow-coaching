## The sticky-note color key (tag every item)
- 🟨 **EXISTS** — a component we already have.
- 🟧 **PLANNED** — an in-house component that's planned, not built yet.
- 🟪 **PARTNER-EXISTS** — a partner component that already exists (Cam, Hannah, Mark deliver it today in Cool Hollow Solutions).
- 🟦 **PARTNER-PLANNED** — a partner component that's planned for the coaching side.

## The three timeline columns
- **FROM** = entry / onboarding (discovery → diagnostic → the 12-week program).
- **JOURNEY** = ongoing community life (the recurring middle, where LTV lives).
- **TO** = the outcome / mature member (business runs without them, ascends to premium).

## What this grid tells us at a glance

**Green (EXISTS) clusters at FROM.** Almost everything we already have lives in the entry column: the analyzer, the 12-week program, the dashboard and forecast templates, the workbooks. Our onboarding is real and buildable today.

**The JOURNEY column is mostly orange (PLANNED).** That's the honest gap and the whole point of the pivot: the recurring middle, the part that actually makes this a business rather than a course, is the part we haven't built. Community platform, weekly Q&A cadence, content library, membership tiers. **This is where the July-to-launch work goes.**

**Partners (purple) are real people who already deliver.** Cam and Hannah do Profit Discovery and Hiring work inside Cool Hollow Solutions today. Productizing them for the coaching side is packaging, not invention. That's a fast win and a credibility anchor.

**Rows 8 and 9 are the retention design.** The positive column is what we promise; the negative column is the list of things that quietly kill a community. The empty-room-at-launch risk (row 9, Journey) is the single biggest one, and it's why a founding cohort should seed the community before we open a public freemium tier.
