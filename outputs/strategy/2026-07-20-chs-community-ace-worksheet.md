## How to read the table

Two things from the sketch are captured in the last two columns.

**Who delivers it** (the crossed-out diagonal boxes in your drawing = partner offers):

- **In-house** — Cool Hollow's own team delivers it.
- **Partner** — delivered by an outside partner, we refer or curate. These are the crossed-out boxes.

**Involvement** (how full the box is drawn = how hands-on our team is):

- **● Full** — our team runs it end to end.
- **◐ Shared** — co-delivered, or we white-label a partner under our brand.
- **○ Light** — a partner runs it, we introduce and hold the relationship only.
