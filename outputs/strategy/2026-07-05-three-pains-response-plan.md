# The Three Trade-Business Pains: Where We Stand and What We Build

Working note, July 5, 2026. Response to Mike's observation that trade businesses show (1) almost no cost/pricing/measurement understanding, (2) weak team engagement and motivation, (3) no real sales and account management function. Prep for the Monday call.

---

## Pain 1: No pricing system — deepen what we have

- Milestone 4 (Profit Discovery Audit) already finds pricing gaps. It is diagnostic. The gap is the install: a pricing SYSTEM the owner runs forward.
- Three missing pieces for a typical trades owner: true job cost (burdened labor, materials with waste, allocated overhead), a forward rate builder (cost + target margin = price, used on every quote), and per-job margin measurement so the system holds past 90 days.
- Why Mike's "double or triple profit" is plausible math: a $2M shop at 5% net makes $100K. A 3-point effective price increase with no volume loss adds $60K straight to profit (+60%). Five points nearly doubles it. Price increases fall almost entirely to the bottom line.
- **Build: the Pricing System Install.** Either a deeper layer in Milestone 4 or an eighth tool: job-cost calculator, rate builder, per-job margin tracker feeding the Monday dashboard.
- **Side benefit:** this is Matt's side-hustle offer. A $2-5K fixed-scope install deliverable now to trade businesses (including Cool Hollow Solutions clients), covering the $2-4K/month debt target, and every install becomes a proof story for the program.

## Pain 2: Team engagement — extend Milestone 3, don't build new

- Milestone 7 covers the structural side (hiring, delegation, leadership) but not motivating the existing crew.
- Three cheap moves for trades: a crew-facing scoreboard (same numbers the owner sees), one simple incentive tied to a number the crew controls (gross margin per job, callbacks, on-time completion), a weekly rhythm where the numbers get said out loud.
- **Build: a crew-facing version of the Milestone 3 dashboard.** One build serves both pains. Scoreboard plus incentive ship together, never separately (measurement without a bonus reads as surveillance and gets resisted).

## Pain 3: No sales and account management function — the real hole

- Nothing in the current seven milestones builds a sales function. Sales is usually the last job the owner still personally holds, so the program's own promise ("a business without you") breaks here without it.
- For trades, the cheapest wins are not new leads: follow-up on unquoted estimates and reactivation of past customers, which most shops never do.
- **Build: Milestone 8, or the first post-graduation community module.** Simple pipeline, quote follow-up cadence, account list with a reactivation rhythm, and a first-salesperson hiring path using Hannah's Milestone 7 templates.
- **Open idea for Monday:** Matt fronts this module (Sam Ovens sales training + real selling experience). The one module where Matt, not Mark, could be the face.
