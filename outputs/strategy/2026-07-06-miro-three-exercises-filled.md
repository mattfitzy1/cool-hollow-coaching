# Miro Board — Three Exercises, Filled for Cool Hollow Coaching

> Mike's board has three frames, each with a blank template and a car-ownership example. This fills all three with our business (Business Without You + the recurring community). Transcribe straight onto the board.
> Prepared 2026-07-06.

**How the car example maps to us:** their "core product" is the car; ours is the transformation (owner gets their time back and finds profit). Everything around it is how we keep earning from that relationship over years. Same logic, our business.

---

## EXERCISE 1 — Package Breakdown (the nested rings)

Four rings, center outward. Center = the one thing they buy. Each ring further out is a looser, longer-term layer of the relationship.

### 🟢 CORE PRODUCT (center)
**Business Without You membership** — the transformation itself: 15 hours back, up to $50,000 in profit found, a business that runs without the owner. Delivered as the ongoing community, entered through the 12-week program.

### 🔵 ACCESSORIES (direct add-ons bolted to the core)
Things that attach right onto the program and make the core win happen:
- Profit Discovery Session (Cam)
- Hiring Sprint (Hannah)
- Hidden-Profit Analyzer tool
- The 5-metric Monday dashboard
- 13-week cash-flow forecast tool
- Client action-plan + workbook templates

### 🟩 EDUCATION & PLANNING (helps them learn, decide, and run it)
The teaching and guidance layer, the reason to stay engaged month to month:
- Core video content library (Mark, Zoom-first)
- Weekly live Q&A with Mark
- Monthly CFO Office Hours (Cam)
- Guest-speaker masterclasses
- Curriculum workbooks + checklists
- Knowledge library (playbooks, templates, how-tos)

### 🔵 MEMORABILIA / ECOSYSTEM (the extended, loyalty, long-tail layer)
The lifestyle-and-belonging ring that keeps them for years:
- Cool Hollow Mastermind (application-only top tier)
- Quarterly business reviews
- Member discounts on partner services (fractional CFO, tax, valuations)
- Peer community + owner network
- Live events / retreats
- Referral rewards + alumni status

## EXERCISE 2 — The Journey (before / journey / after)

Top row = stages across time. The car example used Search → Purchase → Operations → Service → Development → Selling. Ours is the owner's transformation journey.

### Stages across the top (the blue boxes)
1. **Wake-Up** (sees the problem)
2. **Diagnostic** (proves it to themselves)
3. **Onboarding** (the 12-week program)
4. **Convert to Member**
5. **Ongoing Community**
6. **Ascend** (higher tiers / premium)

### BEFORE (left column)
The owner is the single point of failure. 60+ hour weeks, can't take a vacation, everything routes through them, profit leaking they can't see, business unsellable because it *is* them. Exhausted and stuck.

### JOURNEY (fill the two rows at each stage)

**Row A — Dialogue & Communication (the touchpoints/messages at each stage):**
- **Wake-Up:** Instagram pain-point content, "you're the bottleneck" hooks, free Hidden-Profit Analyzer.
- **Diagnostic:** Profit Discovery Session booked, analyzer result, "here's what we found" call.
- **Onboarding:** Weekly 12-week program modules, milestone check-ins, community welcome.
- **Convert:** "Your program's ending, here's what continues" offer, founding-member invite.
- **Ongoing:** Weekly Q&A reminders, CFO Office Hours, monthly wins recap, community threads.
- **Ascend:** Quarterly review invites, mastermind application, premium-service offers.

**Row B — Experience (positive above the line / negative below):**
- **Positive:** fast early win (profit found in week 1–2), time visibly returning, a room full of owners who get it, momentum, Mark accessible weekly, feeling of a business that finally runs.
- **Negative to design out:** feeling sold-to at the convert step, the community feeling empty at launch, unclear what's free vs. paid, homework overload during the 12 weeks, losing momentum after onboarding ends.

### AFTER (right column)
Business runs without them. 15 hours a week back, $50K+ in found profit, five metrics checked in 15 minutes every Monday, a team that owns the seats, a business worth selling. Now an advocate and referrer, staying in the community for the peer network and ongoing edge.

## EXERCISE 3 — Cyclic Identification and Mapping (the concentric arcs)

Four arcs, inner to outer. This is where the recurring-revenue thinking lives: what naturally repeats for an owner, and how we earn from it again and again.

### Arc 1 — Identify Cyclical Purchase Patterns (what naturally recurs for a $1–10M owner)
- **Trigger cycles:** new fiscal year, tax season, hiring season, growth plateau, cash crunch, key employee leaves, planning an exit.
- **Preset recurrence:** monthly financial review, quarterly planning, annual strategy reset.
- **Ongoing needs:** cash-flow management, KPI/dashboard tracking, hiring and team development, constraint-breaking, profit optimization.

### Arc 2 — Opportunities to Shape Those Patterns (steer the cycle to keep them engaged)
- Annual membership renewal built in
- Quarterly business reviews scheduled by default
- "Next milestone" prompts once one is complete
- Seasonal sprints timed to their calendar (Hiring Sprint before busy season, Cash-Flow tune-up before tax time)
- Early-bird cohort / founding-member pricing
- Tier upgrade offered at natural growth moments

### Arc 3 — Additional Opportunities to Monetize the Relationship (premium, partner-delivered)
- Fractional CFO retainer (Cam)
- Tax-strategy service
- Business valuation (quick estimate → full)
- Done-for-you dashboard / systems builds
- Cool Hollow Mastermind tier
- Live events / retreats
- Partner referrals (revenue share)

### Arc 4 — Elements of the Themed Ecosystem Environment (the world that holds it together)
- Member discounts across all partner services
- Knowledge library (playbooks, templates, benchmarks)
- Peer community + owner directory (social layer)
- Gamified milestone tracking
- Referral program + alumni network
- Future: marketplace for third-party tools
- Integration with their own numbers (dashboard pulls live data)
