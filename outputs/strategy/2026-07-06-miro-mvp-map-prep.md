---

## How to set up the board (5 minutes)

1. Create a free Miro account at miro.com.
2. Make one board. Add four column headers across the top as text: **ENTRY (event-based)**, **ONGOING (cyclical)**, **PARTNER-DELIVERED**, **PLATFORM / INFRASTRUCTURE**.
3. Along the bottom, lay out the customer journey as a left-to-right arrow (see the journey section).
4. Drop the sticky notes below into their columns.
5. Color each sticky by the priority key. That colored board *is* your MVP definition.

**Priority color key (use these three colors):**
- 🟢 **GREEN = Critical for launch (the MVP).** Without this, you cannot open the doors.
- 🟡 **YELLOW = Phase 2.** Add within the first 1–3 months once live.
- 🔵 **BLUE = Phase 3 / later.** Real, but not needed to launch.

## The customer journey (the arrow along the bottom)

Left to right, as a row of sticky notes:

1. **Sees content** → Instagram, pain-point hooks.
2. **Tries the free thing** → Hidden-Profit Analyzer or freemium community access.
3. **Buys the entry** → enrolls in the 12-week Business Without You program.
4. **Gets a fast win** → Profit Discovery Session, feels the value early.
5. **Finishes onboarding** → completes the 12 weeks, now bought-in.
6. **Converts to member** → rolls into the ongoing community on a paid tier.
7. **Stays and climbs** → weekly Q&A, office hours, upgrades to higher tiers / premium services / mastermind.

The whole strategic point sits between steps 5 and 6: **the 12 weeks exists to make step 6 automatic.** That transition is the business.

## The MVP, stated plainly (what the green stickies add up to)

**To launch, you need exactly this and nothing more:**

1. A community platform with member access and rooms.
2. Freemium + at least one paid tier, with payments working.
3. GoHighLevel CRM + booking, separate from Solutions.
4. The 12-week program built and deliverable (curriculum is mostly there).
5. One fast finance win at entry (Profit Discovery Session with Cam).
6. A weekly live Q&A with Mark.
7. Enough core video content to fill the first stretch (recorded Zoom-first).

Everything yellow and blue is real, but it's what you add *after* the doors are open. The discipline for the July 14 call is defending that green list and resisting the pull to launch with the whole map.

## One point I'd push back on (bring it up)

Mike's model is right, but the risk in a community-first pivot is **launching to an empty room.** A recurring community with three members feels dead, and dead communities don't retain. So the sequencing matters: you likely want a **founding cohort** (the first 12-week group) to *become* the seed population of the community, rather than launching a public freemium tier into silence. Worth raising with Mike, because it changes what "launch" even means.
