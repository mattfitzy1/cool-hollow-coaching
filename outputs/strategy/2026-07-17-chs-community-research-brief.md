## First sketch of the Cool Hollow community (from the July 17 call)

- **Paid from day one.** Even $100/month changes behavior: "people will be way more likely to be active because they paid for it." Scalable = cheap, not scalable = expensive.
- **Tiers sketch:** $20 / $100 / $500 discussed. The $500 tier could include a monthly live one-to-many call. Matt's instinct: $100-200 entry point.
- **Structure:** topic matrix on the four Cool Hollow domains (People, Strategy, Operations, Financial) crossed with industry rooms (example: finance in HVAC). Start with one group, split as engagement grows.
- **Profiles, searchable.** A member can find "CEO, HVAC, about my size" and DM them. Peer-to-peer answers are a core draw.
- **Seeded discussion groups.** Someone on staff owns keeping content flowing, every day. This is a named job, not a hope.
- **No feed.** People go to Facebook and LinkedIn for feeds. Discussion groups and DMs instead.
- **Courses and content live inside** (LMS in the community, the Senteo pattern). Possibly a book review and reading list corner, which worked in Mike's community.
