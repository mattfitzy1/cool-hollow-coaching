The Big Shift · July 6, With Mike
07 / 13

The biggest change in our thinking: the program isn't the product. The community is.

Working through this with Mike sharpened something important. A one-time $5,000 sale caps revenue at how many new people we enroll each month. A recurring community turns one sale into years of value, and that's what actually gets us to $3M and 500 clients.
