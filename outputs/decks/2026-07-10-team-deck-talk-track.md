**Slide 1 - Title (Progress and Path Forward)**

It's been a while since our last meeting, and a lot has happened since then. Before I start, I want to say thank you to Mike for taking the time to be on this call and working with me on this project.

We both agreed this has huge potential. And I'll be honest, it's proven more complex than I thought, and that's actually got me excited. Because when things are hard, the upside is exponential.

Here's what's on my mind. The next five years, technology is going to change the game. We have to get in front of that curve. AI is driving the cost of information toward zero. The thing consulting used to sell, the decks, the frameworks, the market scans, "here's what your data says," that's being taken over by AI. Pressure-testing a strategy, stress-testing a P&L, generating options, that used to be a fifty-thousand-dollar engagement. Now a competent operator with the right tools does it in an afternoon.

But consulting was never just information. It's judgment, accountability, and implementation, and that's exactly what this company does well. We're not selling information. We're selling transformation, plus accountability, plus tools, plus a community and a network behind it. Cool Hollow Coaching is how we take what Mark does, the actual thing that works, and deliver it to hundreds of owners without Mark in every seat.

So let's get into where I see this going, and I want your honest thoughts on it.

**Slide 2 - Why This Exists**

Cool Hollow Solutions is steady. Thirty-five clients, sixty thousand a month. A strong, proven book of business. But it's capped, and it's capped by the calendar. There's only so much of Mark to go around.

The fix is one-to-many. Package the same expertise, the same team, into something that scales without needing more of Mark's hours per client.

**Slide 3 - The Vision**

Business Without You. We get the owner out of the way of their own business. Our audience is owner-operators doing one to ten million a year who are the bottleneck everything runs through.

The promise is fifteen hours back in their week, plus up to fifty thousand in profit found already sitting inside the business. The exact headline is still being sharpened, that's honest, but that's the shape of it.

Long-term goal, three million a year, five hundred clients. And the philosophy underneath all of it: 10x is easier than 2x.

Here's the thing I keep coming back to. There are twenty-four-year-olds building eight-figure companies from a laptop, with the exact same tools we're going to talk about today. No team, no track record, no CFO. Just nerve and good timing. We have all of that already, plus the hard part they don't have: Mark's name, a real finance team, thirty-five clients who trust us. That's why I don't think three million is the ceiling. I think it's the floor, and I don't see a reason we can't build toward ten million if we move fast enough.

**Slide 5 - What's Been Built: The Tools**

Here's the part I'm proud of. Every single milestone has a real working tool behind it. Actual software, not slideware. All seven are built and backtested against real client P&Ls, checked until the numbers matched to the cent.

These tools are built for value and efficiency. They give us real insight into a client's numbers, so we can help them faster and see exactly where the issues are, instead of guessing.

The next few slides show you three of them actually running.

**Slide 6 - Tool Example: The Hidden-Profit Analyzer (live and public)**

This one's free and live right now, anyone can use it. An owner uploads their P&L and gets this back. Here's a real anonymized run, a restoration company. Revenue about 1.4 million, looks healthy on the surface. But the operating margin is minus five percent. Costs are running at a hundred and five percent of revenue. This business is losing money and the owner probably doesn't feel it yet.

That's exactly the point. The tool doesn't diagnose, it surfaces the signal and opens the conversation. That's our free front door. Someone sees a number like that, and they want to talk to us.

**Slide 7 - Tool Example: The Monday Morning Dashboard (Milestone 3)**

Cash on hand is red, that's the fire. But look at the two yellows, backlog and lead flow, those are the early warning before it becomes a red.

**Slide 11 - The Big Shift, July 6 With Mike**

So the twelve-week program becomes the front door. It delivers a fast win and turns a buyer into a long-term member. ## Delivery notes
- The young-founder line now lives on Slide 3, near the top, since that's where you naturally reach for it. Just make sure you don't repeat it again later, say it once and let it land.
- Keep the number straight: $3M / 500 clients is what's printed on your slides. $10M is your spoken ambition, said once, as "I don't see why we can't build toward ten million." Don't let it drift to a different number mid-sentence.
- Slides 6, 7, 8 are your proof. Slow down on the ugly numbers: "105% of revenue," "$18,400 sitting overdue."
- Slide 11: say it forward, not backward. "The program isn't the product. The community is." Not the other way around.
- Curriculum module grouping (Slide 4): M1-3, M4-5, M6, M7. Say it in that order every time so it doesn't tangle.
- It's Hormozi, not Somoza. Say his name slowly a few times beforehand if it's catching in your mouth.
- Slow way down on Slide 17. Pause before "That's why I'm standing here," then move to the ask.
- If nerves hit, look at Mark on Slide 2, and at Cam and Hannah when you name their milestones on Slide 16.
- You don't have to hit these words exactly. Know the beat of each slide and let it come out as you.
